# Current frame and solid-mark reference

The legacy enclosing Square/Circle variants have been removed. Current identities such as Square and Circle-shaped Info are authored icons, not generic frames around arbitrary glyphs. Do not regenerate the old frame collection or silently wrap current icons.

Read [ICON_RULES.md](ICON_RULES.md) for current source treatment and part-by-part decisions. Preserve each authored Filled contour/counter and its actual Outline relationship. Plus/Minus/X and other solid marks retain their current source geometry. A future enclosing-frame request needs separate owner-authorized artwork and verification; no historical frame formula is the current default.

[MORPH.md](MORPH.md) owns the five unframed transformations and their existing timing. Packaging changes do not authorize motion redesign or new visual approval.
